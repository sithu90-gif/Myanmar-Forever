# MyanmarForever 1.1.0: Undead route expansion

This update continues the approved Deathknell style through Brill and Tirisfal Glades. The new inventory has **94 quest additions**: 50 Tirisfal story/side quests, 30 originating class or immediate capital follow-ups, and 14 local Camping/profession introductions. The 24 released Deathknell records remain unchanged.

The combined Undead scope reaches approximately **levels 1–13**. The new records have quest levels 4–13: the level-4 priest introduction overlaps the starter range, while seven level-13 records finish local story or Rogue chains. These ranges describe the supported routes, not a guarantee that every quest or live branch at those levels is translated.

## Route and class scope

- Brill, Cold Hearth Manor, Garren's Haunt, Agamand Mills and the family crypt, Scarlet Crusade chains, plague research, the Whispering Forest and Bandarion Keep.
- Supported early Warrior, Rogue, Mage, Priest, Warlock and Paladin routes, including appropriate Undercity handoffs.
- Eleanor Shackleton's local Camping introduction and profession-specific tutorials.
- The delivery to Silverpine is included as a handoff. The Silverpine questing zone is outside this update.

The seven level-13 closures are 408, 1886, 1898, 1899, 1978, 95328 and 99156. Class, profession and quest prerequisites still govern which records a character can accept.

## English evidence and exclusions

Inventory reconciliation uses installed QuestieDB 1.0.11 plus public 60.tools and ForeverDB zone lists. All included records were checked at Wowhead Forever for missing dialogue states. The four Camping records missing from the primary database (97952, 97954, 97958 and 97961) have English evidence from Wowhead. UA_Forever 0.23.0 is a pinned secondary identity/state comparison, not the English authority or a claim of today's upstream version.

Quest 5658, Touch of Weakness, remains excluded: its local Undercity metadata conflicts with the available English Troll/zeppelin referral text. A current live capture or corrected English source is needed before enabling it. Higher-level, event, riding/mount and unrelated starting-region branches are explicitly listed in the inventory; database presence alone does not establish live eligibility.

Available source fields are 94 titles, 91 offers, 91 objectives, 70 progress replies and 94 completions: **440 fields**. Missing source states remain empty. Existing source holds from earlier releases remain in force.

## Translation and validation

All enabled new fields pass through DeepL MY and source-aware assisted editing. The established local pronunciation map controls retained English identities. DeepL Free does not support an external glossary; new phonetic spellings remain assisted editorial proposals.

Per-quest records distinguish source-aware editing, limited integration review, machine checks, native proofreading and live verification. Full native Myanmar proofreading and current live-client/controller verification remain pending. Automatic local quest capture continues to collect missing or mismatched dialogue for review.

All 16 Lua regression groups pass. Validation also checks source/review hashes, placeholders and numeric quantities, annotations, every runtime Unicode round trip and shaped glyph, both TOCs and ZIP contents. Each of the 440 new fields has its exact English input and raw DeepL output hash verified. Root integration review sampled 10 quest records / 45 fields; this is a sample, not independent certification of every field. All 852 prior quest records and all 176 files in the preserved released build snapshot were verified unchanged.
