MyanmarForever 1.1.0
Short first-introduction meanings — 8 November 2026

Version 1 contains 46 curated faction, class, race, rank and game-concept meanings,
including Skyborne. Important English identities retain Myanmar pronunciation.
The first successful live display of an eligible term adds one short meaning.
Later encounters use pronunciation alone. Ordinary NPC/place names do not receive
blanket explanations or extra quest advice.

Examples:
Horde (ဟော်ဒ် — မျိုးနွယ်စုပေါင်းအင်အားစု)
Warrior (ဝေါရီယာ — အနီးကပ်တိုက်ခိုက်တဲ့ စစ်သည်)
Skyborne (စကိုင်းဘောန်း — ကောင်းကင်ဒေသမှ အယ်လ်ဖ်မျိုးနွယ်)

Introduction history is per character, using the existing SavedVariables names.
Static and dynamic class/race labels share the same history. Skyborne is its own
race identity, separate from Undead. Previews show introductions for review and
never consume live history. A held/missing state, title mismatch, hidden or failed
render and English debug display do not consume it either. The current displayed
section remains stable while resizing or redrawing. Window reset does not clear
term history. Reload/logout saves it normally.

Close WoW fully and replace the complete Myanmar_Forever_NorthshireTest addon
folder. Keep the rebuilt font and catalog together; do not mix versions.

Automated source, glyph and Lua tests verify data integrity and introduction
lifecycle. Full native proofreading and current live Skyborne/controller checks
remain pending; these are distinct from the version 1.0.0 release number.
