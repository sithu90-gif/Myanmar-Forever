Myanmar Forever 0.6.1 beta candidate

1. Quit WoW completely. Save a backup of your current addon folder and SavedVariables.
2. Extract the ZIP. Copy the single Myanmar_Forever_NorthshireTest folder into your
   WoW Interface/AddOns folder. Keep that exact folder name.
3. Replace the whole folder. Never mix Catalogs/Northshire.lua and
   fonts/NorthshireShaped.ttf from different versions.
4. Start WoW again and enable Myanmar Forever. A reload alone may keep a cached font.
5. Test your actual accepted quest ID, English title and dialogue state. Preview
   commands only demonstrate packaged text; they do not establish live availability.

Read CANDIDATE-SUMMARY.txt, Full-Catalog-Review.html and TESTING.txt before approving
this candidate. Eleven held fields deliberately use the normal game fallback.
This package is not published. Native Myanmar/live/controller review is pending.
