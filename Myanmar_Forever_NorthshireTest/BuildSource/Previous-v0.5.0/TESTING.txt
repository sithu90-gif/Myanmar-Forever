MyanmarForever v0.5.0-beta — live test checklist

These steps have NOT been run in a live client. Start with a full client restart.
Use Orc and Troll characters as appropriate. Keep the normal English quest
window available for comparison; previews alone do not verify live behavior.

1. FIRST OPEN: Speak to a Valley of Trials NPC, or preview /mmforever 788.
   Myanmar must appear immediately without pressing A+ or A-. Check stacked
   marks, clipping, English names, punctuation and Arabic digits.
2. STARTER ROUTE: Compare actual offer, objective, progress and completion
   text for 788, 789 and custom quest 97279. Continue toward Sen'jin (826),
   Razor Hill (784), and The Crossroads (871) when those quests are available.
   Compare names, directions, creature types and every objective quantity.
3. CLASS INTROS: Check your own available class introduction:
   Orc: Warrior 2383, Hunter 3087, Rogue 3088, Shaman 3089, Warlock 3090.
   Troll: Warrior 3065, Hunter 3082, Rogue 3083, Shaman 3084, Priest 3085, Mage 3086.
   These are introductions, not a claim of complete class-chain coverage.
4. IDENTITIES: In quest 6394, verify player name and race on both Orc and Troll.
   Check class substitutions in 96875 if available. Include a player name
   containing punctuation when possible. Do not interpret captured player
   names as universal source text.
5. HOLDS: Quest 842 completion and 3083 progress must keep native English.
   Verify that each live state is captured. Capture 842 with male and female
   characters to resolve the reference's combined replies. Capture 3083 on
   more than one Rogue to confirm the player-name substitution.
6. SOURCE QUIRKS: Check 4641 progress (scorpid wording), 3065 offer (parchment
   versus Simple Tablet), and 890's Drizzlek/Drizzlik spelling against live text.
   Quest 97279 has no reference progress; 926 has only completion; repeatable
   turn-ins 889 and 5042–5046 have progress/completion but no offer draft.
7. LONG TEXT: Preview /mmforever 784 or another long offer. Scroll to both ends,
   resize narrow/wide and change text size. Check clipping, overlap and errors.
8. INPUTS: Assign spare inputs under Key Bindings > Myanmar Forever. Verify
   all six actions. Close during an NPC dialogue transition; it must stay
   closed until explicitly reopened or the next dialogue state occurs.
   If your setup supports gamepad bindings, repeat scroll/close/toggle and
   confirm existing movement/combat inputs still work.
9. SAVE AND FALLBACK: Move the window, change text size, /reload, and reopen.
   Verify settings and existing captures persist. Open an unlisted quest:
   the reader must hide, native English must remain usable, and the state
   must be captured locally.

Log quest ID, dialogue stage, live English and Myanmar screenshots, client build,
class, race and input setup. Mark captured text as player-substituted.
/reload or logout before copying the addon's SavedVariables capture file.
Do not mark a draft verified based only on its /mmforever preview.
