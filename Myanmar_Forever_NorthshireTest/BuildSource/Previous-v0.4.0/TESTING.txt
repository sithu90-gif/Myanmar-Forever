MyanmarForever v0.4.0-beta — live test checklist

These steps have NOT been run in a live client. Start with a full client restart.
Use a Night Elf character and retain the normal English quest window for comparison.

1. FIRST OPEN: Speak to a starter NPC, or preview /mmforever 456. Check that
   Myanmar is visible immediately without pressing A+ or A-. Check stacked marks,
   clipped ascenders/descenders, English names, punctuation and Arabic digits.
2. QUEST STATES: Play available quests in Shadowglen, Teldrassil and Darkshore.
   Compare offers, objectives, progress and rewards with the live English text.
   Useful previews: 97977, 934, 983, 4763 and your class introduction 3116–3120.
   Previewing confirms the catalog only; actual NPC dialogue confirms routing.
3. IDENTITY: Confirm player name/class/race appear correctly. Check quest 3118
   progress keeps the native text and records a capture; it is deliberately held.
4. LONG TEXT: Open /mmforever 4763. Scroll to both ends, resize narrow/wide,
   then change text size. Check for clipping, overlap, runaway scroll or errors.
5. BINDINGS: Assign spare keys under Myanmar Forever. Verify all six actions.
   Close during an NPC dialogue transition; the reader must stay closed until
   explicitly reopened or the next dialogue state occurs.
6. GAMEPAD: If your setup exposes gamepad bindings, assign spare buttons and
   repeat scroll/close/toggle. Confirm your existing movement/combat inputs work.
7. SAVE: Move the window, change text size, /reload, and reopen a quest.
   Verify settings persist and existing captures are still present.
8. FALLBACK: Open a quest not in the package. The English quest remains usable;
   the Myanmar window should hide and the live state should be captured locally.

Log each issue with quest ID, stage, live English screenshot, Myanmar screenshot,
client build, class, race and input setup. Mark captures as player-substituted.
/reload or logout before copying the addon's SavedVariables capture file.
Do not mark a draft verified based only on its /mmforever preview.
