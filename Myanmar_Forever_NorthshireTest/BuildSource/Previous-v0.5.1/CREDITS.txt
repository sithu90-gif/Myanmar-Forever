refines eight older fields, and adds live English quest-title validation.

Translations are AI-assisted drafts; native Myanmar review remains pending.

UA_Forever 0.18.5-beta was reviewed for quest IDs, English titles, field coverage,
numeric differences and title-validation behavior. Its title-validation approach
informed the new guard; this is not a wholesale port of its UI translation system.

public 60.tools, Wowhead and ForeverChanges quest references. Public database

BuildSource/Previous-v0.3.0, Previous-v0.4.0 and Previous-v0.5.0.
