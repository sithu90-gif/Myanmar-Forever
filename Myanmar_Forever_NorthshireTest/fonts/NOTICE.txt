Latin outlines: the project's v0.3.0-beta MM Northshire Shaped font, itself a
modified Noto Sans Myanmar font distributed under the same SIL OFL license.

Private-use glyphs contain pre-shaped Myanmar clusters, the em is adjusted
for mark height, and OpenType layout tables are removed from the runtime font.
